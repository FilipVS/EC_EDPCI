Information Day on the European Defence Industry Programme (EDIP Info Day) – 24 September 2026

2 October, 2026

We would like to inform you that the Ministry of National Defence of the Hellenic Republic is planning to organize an Information Day on the European Defence Industry Program (EDIP Info Day), which will take place on September 24, 2026, at the Armed Forces Officers’ Club (LAED) in Athens.

The event will be combined with the Greek Cypriot Defence Industry Day and will focus on the latest developments regarding the EDIP programme and the European Defence Projects of Common Interest (EDPCI).

At the same time, the aim is to highlight opportunities for cooperation between the Greek and Cypriot defence industries, as well as to further strengthen bilateral cooperation in the fields of defence, research, technology and innovation.

OEB members who are active in the field of defence and have related products and/or services are invited to consider participating in this event.

Interested businesses are kindly requested to send to the e-mail defence.industry@mod.gov.cy the details of their representatives who will participate (name, title/position and company) no later than Monday, August 31, 2026, for the purposes of timely planning of the Cypriot participation and coordination with the organizers.

For more information or clarifications, those interested can contact the above e-mail address, the Defense Industry Development Sector, Armaments & Defense Capabilities Development Directorate.
