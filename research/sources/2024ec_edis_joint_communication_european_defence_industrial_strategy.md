## Raw summary (EDPCI-relevant)
- Section 2.1.2, "From strategic programming to Projects of common interest": the Union "should identify, within the EU Capability Development Priorities, **European Defence Projects of Common Interest** on which to focus efforts and resources", to secure access to strategic domains and contested spaces, "including by developing appropriate systems that would serve as **European defence infrastructure of common interest and use**". **"By 2035, the EU should have in place, or at a mature stage of development, projects for capabilities that are European by nature"** — subject to Member States' agreement, examples: integrated European **air and missile defence**, **Space Domain Awareness**, a **network of cyber defensive capabilities**, and **maritime and underwater protective assets**.
- A proposed EU-level joint programming and procurement function would jointly identify possible Projects of common interest, building on CDP, CARD and PESCO.
- The Commission proposes "through EDIP, the launch of European Defence Projects of common interest" to protect free access to contested areas (cyber, space, air, maritime).
- **SEAP** (Structure for European Armament Programme) described as a vehicle for cooperative life-cycle armament programmes: standardised procedures, increased EDIP funding rate under conditions, simplified procurement, VAT exemption where jointly owned, export-approach bonus, and the option to issue debt titles; EDIP contributes to SEAP functioning.

## Key conclusions
- ✅ **The original 2024 EDPCI concept was "European-by-nature" infrastructure-type capabilities (air/missile defence, space awareness, cyber defence, maritime/underwater assets)**, with a 2035 horizon. The five projects identified in Sept 2026 differ: no cyber project; drones and Eastern Flank Watch were added. This helps explain why the Council texts read as capability areas rather than product lines.
- ✅ The SEAP is the intended legal vehicle for long-term cooperative programmes; the final Regulation lets an EDPCI be established within a SEAP (Art. 35(12)).
