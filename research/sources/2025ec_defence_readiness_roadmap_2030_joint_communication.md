# Joint Communication JOIN(2025) 27 final — "Preserving Peace – Defence Readiness Roadmap 2030" (16 Oct 2025)

- Source: European Commission and High Representative; https://defence-industry-space.ec.europa.eu/document/download/9db42c04-15c2-42e1-8364-60afb0073e68_en?filename=Joint-Communication+_Defence-Readiness-Roadmap-2030.pdf (fetched 2026-10-01; PDF creation 16 Oct 2025, 16 pp.)
- Evidence tier: ✅ official EU policy document (a Communication: sets proposals and milestones, not binding law)
- Attachment: `attachments/2025ec_defence_readiness_roadmap_2030_joint_communication.pdf` — ✅ read.

## Raw summary
- **Member States Capability Coalitions**: Member States complete the formation of coalitions per priority area, with lead and co-lead nations, objectives, governance and collaborative projects; open to other Member States joining later; EDA facilitates through capability expert groups; the Commission ensures the link between coalitions and EU funding. **"Each coalition may use one or several of the tools available in EDIP [footnote: e.g. EDPCI or SEAP when in force] to trigger targeted EU support for flagship projects"**. Milestones: coalitions, leads and plans by **Q1 2026**; initial data on EU defence industrial capacity per priority area by **mid-2026**; projects launched in H1 2026.
- **Readiness Flagships** (Drone Defence Initiative, Eastern Flank Watch, Air Shield, Space Shield): Member States decide objectives, task distribution and national budget allocation; **Commission acts as facilitator with a "one-stop-shop" giving technical assistance to Member States**; participating Member States to agree a coordination arrangement **by Spring 2026**. EFW/Drone milestones: launch Q1 2026; initial capacity end 2026; Drone Initiative functional end 2027; EFW functional end 2028. Eastern Flank Watch objective lists multi-domain surveillance, drone/counter-drone, **electronic warfare**, precision strike.
- **Industry dialogue**: "Commission, in close cooperation with lead nations of the capability coalitions, **expands the strategic dialogue with industry in first half of 2026 and hosts the first annual Defence Industrial Summit by mid-2026**" (summit to involve governments, EU actors, primes, SMEs, mid-caps and tech innovators); Commission overview of industrial capacity ramp-up needed per agreed capability area, **starting mid-2026**; **Tech Alliances for Defence** to connect innovators with defence users in Member States, by end 2025; critical-raw-material risk analysis and action plan H2 2026.

## Key conclusions
- ✅ **The lead is real**: the Roadmap itself schedules industry dialogue "in close cooperation with lead nations of the capability coalitions" (H1 2026), an annual Defence Industrial Summit (mid-2026) and an industrial-capacity overview (from mid-2026). It does **not** name an EDPCI-specific supplier channel, nor a company registry.
- ✅ The Commission's one-stop-shop service is for **Member States**, not for companies. Coalitions are **Member State-led** and may use EDPCI/SEAP to trigger EU support.
- ⚠️ Whether these milestones were actually delivered on time is **not shown** by this document (dated Oct 2025).
