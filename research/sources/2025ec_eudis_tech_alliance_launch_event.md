## Raw summary
Morning: launch of the EUDIS Tech Alliances to foster dialogue between Member States and defence innovators; **emerging defence players pitched solutions to Member State representatives, including leaders of the drone / counter-drone capability coalition**. Afternoon: Defence Industry Roundtable with Commissioner Kubilius and **40+ defence companies from the EU and Ukraine** on drone and counter-drone capacities and scale-up.

## Key conclusions
- ✅ **A concrete, documented channel where companies meet coalition leaders exists — for drones/counter-drone, and oriented to emerging/innovator companies.** It predates the EDPCIs (Nov 2025) and is not described as an EDPCI mechanism.
- ⚠️ Unknown: whether further Tech Alliance rounds exist for other EDPCI domains (air & missile defence, space, eastern flank) or how a company joins.
