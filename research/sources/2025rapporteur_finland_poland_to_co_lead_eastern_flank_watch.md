## Raw summary
- Leaders of **Finland, Sweden, Estonia, Latvia, Poland, Bulgaria, Romania and Lithuania** met in Helsinki for the "first-ever Eastern Flank Summit" to discuss operationalising the Eastern Flank Watch. Finnish PM Orpo: "Finland is ready to take the lead with Poland on the Eastern Flank Watch." The eight had met Commissioner Kubilius in September (with Hungary and Slovakia) on drone incursions.
- The group "plans to establish a European Defence Common Project (EDPCI) under the €1.5 billion … EDIP", covering ground combat, drone defence, air and missile defence, border/critical-infrastructure protection, military mobility and counter-mobility, strategic enablers (per their joint statement). Overall cost "remains unclear"; Tusk urged "political pressure" on the Commission for funding; real test is the next EU budget (EUR 131 bn defence and space, earmarked by the Commission).
- Context at the time: the EU27 had **not** endorsed any flagship; Italy and others cautioned against an eastern-only focus; a January review of priorities was expected.

## Key conclusions
- ✅ (as reported) **A political commitment in Dec 2025 that Finland and Poland would co-lead Eastern Flank Watch**, with six other countries.
- ⚠️ **It is not an official lead designation:** the Council decision of 28 Sep 2026 names no lead nation (confirmed from the Council text and a secondary commentary), and the May 2026 expression of interest was submitted by six states (LT, FI, EE, LV, PL, SE — Lithuanian MoD). Do not cite "Finland and Poland lead EFW" as a formal fact.
- ✅ **Czechia is not among the eight leaders** at Helsinki nor among the six May 2026 initiators; the Council annex lists it only as a participant.
- Out of date as a description of the final project: the Dec 2025 scope and country group differ from the identified EDPCI.
