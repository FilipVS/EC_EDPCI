## Raw summary
- AOBP is the EDF **National Focal Point** (Kristýna Helm, Daniel Ždych; edf@aobp.cz).
- **EDF 2026 deadline: 29 Sep 2026, 17:00** (already passed as of 1 Oct 2026). EDF is the R&D fund — a different programme family from EDIP.
- **MPO is the new administrator of Letters of Intent (LoI) and security guarantees** for EDF proposals (previously MoD). Process: fill questionnaire → informal check with JUDr. Matěj Benda, Ph.D. (matej.benda@mpo.gov.cz) → send signed form via data box (ID bxtaaw4) → MPO asks the General Staff of the Czech Army whether results are usable → MPO prepares LoI. Security guarantees: 30-day inter-ministry comment round (MoI, MFA, NBÚ, NÚKIB) and Ownership Control Declaration.
- Czech national co-funding **PRODEF** (administered by TAČR, funded via MoD): up to 50 % of the Czech applicant's *own co-financing* in EDF development actions.
- EDIP section: generic summary (EUR 1.5 bn; IRA >EUR 700 m; "35–100 %" funding depending on call; applications from 30 Apr 2026). No Czech-specific EDIP process stated.

## Key conclusions
- ✅(by an association that relays official procedure) **Matěj Benda is named as the working contact for LoI/security guarantees at MPO, with a published e-mail and data-box ID.** This is the first source that gives MPO's department an operational role and a contact channel (for EDF, not EDIP/EDPCI).
