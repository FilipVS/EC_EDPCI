## Raw summary
- Status banner: **"Uzavřená výzva" (closed call).** Call announced 19 Jun 2026; receipt opened 17 Jul 2026 10:00; **receipt ended 11 Aug 2026 00:01.**
- Notice of 11 Aug 2026: closed because submitted applications reached **300 % of the allocation**, as set in the call. **169 applications, CZK 5,632,256,725.15 requested.**
- Scope shown: digital/deep tech (semiconductors, AI, quantum, connectivity, navigation, robotics); **clean tech (solar, batteries, hydrogen, heat pumps, CO₂ capture)**; biotech; **defence tech (air defence, drones, cyber, military mobility)**.
- Eligible: SMEs, large enterprises, research organisations (as partners). Grant CZK 10–150 m per project; support 45–80 % small, 35–75 % medium, 25–65 % large enterprise, 85 % research organisations. Projects must be at **TRL 4+** before start; must meet one of two STEP conditions (innovative/emerging/cutting-edge element, or reducing strategic dependency).
- Contact: programy@apiagentura.gov.cz; hotline +420 800 800 777 (Mon–Fri 9–13).
