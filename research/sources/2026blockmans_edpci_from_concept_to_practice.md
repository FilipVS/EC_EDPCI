## Raw summary (EDPCI-relevant)
- Frames EDPCIs as modelled on IPCEIs; examines four early flagships (Drone Defence Initiative, Eastern Flank Watch, Air Shield, Space Shield — the Commission's Sept 2025 scoping paper), i.e. precursors of DECODER, EFW, EU-FIAMD, SPACE. IMSD is not among them.
- Draft award criteria include fostering armament cooperation "involving … to a significant extent, SMEs, small mid-caps and other mid-caps as recipients, as subcontractors or as other undertakings in the supply chain". Argues EDPCIs can be a pathway for SMEs/mid-caps into defence supply chains.
- Financing: argues EDPCIs should be EU-financed public goods; EU grants up to 100 % possible; EDPCIs may be set up in a SEAP; analyst speculated EUR 225 m (30 % of EUR 1.5 bn) could be available for EDPCIs and doubted that would launch flagships; European Court of Auditors (quoted) warned the projects' horizon exceeds EDIP's 2-year period and long-term financing is needed. Notes unclear roles of SAFE loans and EIB; future-MFF defence/space request of EUR 131 bn (a proposal ⚠️).
- Technology remarks: drone defence implies sensor networks (acoustic, **radar**, optical/signal detection); Air Shield should push European **sensors, effectors, C2** and electronic warfare/directed energy. Batteries appear only in the IPCEI-lessons section (the battery IPCEI), not as an EDPCI topic.
- Recommends strict cross-border focus with SME/mid-cap involvement; mentions that the "Readiness Roadmap" foresees **expanding "capability coalition" dialogues with industry in mid-2026**.

## Key conclusions
- ⚠️ Context only; it **cannot** establish how EDPCI funding works now. Two of its numbers are superseded by official texts: EDPCI money is **EUR 300 m** (Reg. Art. 3(4): 25 % of EUR 1.2 bn) + EUR 25 m, not EUR 225 m.
- ✅ The SME/mid-cap cooperation hook is real in the final text too (Reg. Art. 35(2)(a)(i): "cross-border cooperation, including with SMEs and mid-caps") — an eligibility criterion for the *project*, not an entry mechanism for a company.
- ✅ SEAP route confirmed in final text (Art. 35(12)); long-term financing beyond 2027 is an acknowledged gap.
- ⚠️ Lead worth checking: **"capability coalition" dialogues with industry (mid-2026)** — could be the channel where suppliers meet EDPCI participants. Not verified in any official document.
- No battery/RF statement for EDPCIs in this study; radar/sensor/EW mentions are generic ⚠️.
