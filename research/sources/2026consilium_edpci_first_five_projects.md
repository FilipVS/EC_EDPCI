## Summary (what it says)
- Council "today approved five European Defence Projects of Common Interest (EDPCIs)".
- EDPCIs were "introduced by the European Defence Industry Programme (EDIP) adopted by the EU in 2025": collaborative industrial projects for capabilities critical to Union security/defence, too large or complex for single countries.
- The five "are now eligible to receive EU funding through the EDIP. The funding will enable the projects' initial deployment and lay the foundation for possible further funding via the upcoming European Competitiveness Fund."
- Quote from Helen McEntee, Irish Minister for Defence; mentions "partners Norway and Ukraine".
- The five: DECODER (drones/counter-drone), IMSD (maritime/seabed), SPACE (early warning sats, SATCOM, ISR, PNT, NAVWAR, rapid replacement launch), EU-FIAMD (integrated air & missile defence incl. early warning, C2), EFW (eastern border, multi-domain). One-paragraph scope each; **no budgets, no country lists** in the press release.
- Timeline: 16 Feb 2026 Commission call for expressions of interest; nine proposals; 3 Jul 2026 Commission proposed five (after HR and EDA opinion).
- Links listed: Council implementing decision; Commission page "Commission proposes five joint defence projects…"; EDIP final approval press release (8 Dec 2025).

## Key conclusions
- ✅ EDPCI is an **EDIP** (Reg. (EU) 2025/2643) instrument, not an EDF/EDIRPA/ASAP item.
- ✅ Being an EDPCI = *eligibility* for EDIP funding; the release states no amounts, no calls, no deadlines.
- ✅ Possible follow-on funding is the future European Competitiveness Fund (not yet existing → "not yet published").
