# Council implementing decision identifying EDPCIs — draft ST 12742/26 (14 Sep 2026)

- Source: Council doc 12742/26, Interinstitutional File 2026/0192(NLE), dated Brussels 14 Sep 2026, marked **LIMITE**. PDF creation date 29 Sep 2026.
- Attachment: `attachments/2026consilium_edpci_implementing_decision_draft_st12742.pdf` (user-supplied, text layer). ✅ full text read.
- Tier: official EU document ✅ — **but a pre-adoption draft** ("Decision (EU) 2026/… of …", date and signatory blank). The adopted/OJ text may differ; verify.

## Summary
- Legal basis: Reg. (EU) 2025/2643 of 16 Dec 2025 (EDIP Regulation), Art. 35(3); on Commission proposal.
- Recitals: call for expressions of interest launched 16 Feb 2026; **nine proposals submitted by Member States by 29 May 2026**; HR and EDA consulted (Art. 35(5)(b)); Commission verified all MS, associated countries and, where relevant, Ukraine were informed and could participate (Art. 35(5)(c)); five proposed.
- Recital 6: Council agreed with understanding that Commission will regularly assess further EDPCIs and **launch a second call for expressions of interest before end April 2027**.
- Recital 7: Commission may participate in EDPCIs; participating MS may involve HR and EDA as observers.
- Art. 2: enters into force the day after OJ publication.
- Annex, per project: objectives, participating countries "as of the date of adoption", estimated overall financial size (see `topics/pci_comparison.md` for the table).

## Key conclusions
- ✅ **Czechia is listed as participating in 4 of 5**: DECODER, SPACE, EU-FIAMD, EFW. **Not in IMSD.**
- ✅ Participation is **by country** (states). No companies, lead entities or consortia are named anywhere in the decision.
- ✅ "Estimated overall financial size" are estimates of the whole EDPCI, not EU funding commitments; the decision contains no EU budget figure, instrument, call or deadline.
- ✅ A **second call for expressions of interest** (for new EDPCIs, MS-submitted) is intended before end Apr 2027 — a route for Czech MS-level proposals, not a company call.
- Scope text mentions "sensors, effectors and command and control" (FIAMD), SIGINT/SATCOM/PNT/NAVWAR (SPACE), C4ISTAR/counter-mobility (EFW). **No document uses the words battery, radar, RF.** Battery/RF fit is therefore inference ⚠️.
