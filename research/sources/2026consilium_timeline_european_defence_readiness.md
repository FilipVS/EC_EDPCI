## Raw summary (EDPCI-relevant entries)
- **28 Sep 2026:** Council approves five EDPCIs; "now eligible to receive EU funding through the European defence industry programme".
- **19 Jun 2026 (European Council):** leaders stress ramping up readiness by 2030; welcome progress in priority capability areas "particularly on drone and counter-drone systems and early-warning, air defence and long-range precision strike"; stress defence of all borders "including on the EU's Eastern flank".
- **19 Mar 2026 (European Council):** leaders "welcomed progress on **capability coalitions**, joint development and procurement as regards drone and counter-drone systems, and initiatives to protect Europe's Eastern flank".
- **18 Dec 2025 (European Council):** leaders "reviewed the ongoing work at national level, **including capability coalitions and priority areas, with a view to launching projects in the first half of 2026**."
- Other entries (not EDPCI): SAFE loans — e.g. **10 Apr 2026: Council approved SAFE financial assistance for Czechia, maximum loan EUR 2.06 bn** (and France); AGILE (EUR 115 m SME defence-innovation programme, operational from early 2027); military mobility; Canada in SAFE; national escape clauses.

## Key conclusions
- ✅ **Official confirmation that the capability coalitions exist and are making progress**, with drone/counter-drone and Eastern-flank work the most advanced (European Council, 19 Mar and 19 Jun 2026). Consistent with the pattern of the five identified EDPCIs.
- ✅ The timeline lists **no Defence Industrial Summit, industry dialogue or EDPCI industry event** (it is a Council/European Council record, so absence is weak evidence only).
- ⚠️ Adjacent, not EDPCI: Czechia has a SAFE loan allocation of up to EUR 2.06 bn (10 Apr 2026). The EDIP Work Programme says EDPCI funding proposals must indicate "links with SAFE plans" — a possible national link to ask MPO/MoD about (inference).
