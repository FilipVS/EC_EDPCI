## Raw summary
- Held **23 & 24 June 2026**, Cercle Royal Gaulois, Brussels; theme "From Vision to Readiness: Delivering Europe's Defence".
- Speakers listed as "invited": Commissioner Kubilius (keynote), DG DEFIS Director-General Timo Pesonen and two deputy DGs, EDA CEO André Denk, ASD Secretary General Camille Grand, Defence Adviser to President von der Leyen, **Rear Admiral Francisco Javier Vázquez Sanz, "EU Priority Capability Areas Coordinator, Spanish MoD"**, MEPs incl. Tomáš Zdechovský, EUSPA, NATO.
- Programme: panels on scaling defence manufacturing, European single market for defence, Ukraine lessons, naval capability ("The EDA, Member States, and industry are jointly defining…"), space, interoperability, EU–Türkiye cooperation. One session is "a privileged gathering bringing together defence industry primes, SMEs, Member State representatives, and institutional players to engage directly with EDA leadership on … capability, armament, and planning priorities" (exclusive). 
- **No session titled on EDPCIs, flagships or capability coalitions;** EDIP appears only in the text of the Türkiye panel.

## Key conclusions
- ⚠️ **Timing and attendance fit the Roadmap's "Defence Industrial Summit by mid-2026"** (Commission leadership present, industry and Member State representatives, mid-year), **but nothing on these pages says the Commission hosts it or that it is that Summit** — the organiser is a commercial company and the Roadmap says the *Commission* "hosts" its summit. I therefore cannot confirm or exclude that this is the event the Roadmap meant. (Correction of my earlier flat statement that it was "a private conference, not the Commission's Summit": that was too strong.)
- ✅ The programme has **no EDPCI-specific supplier session**.
- ⚠️ A named person who coordinates "EU Priority Capability Areas" at the Spanish MoD appears — a possible lead for how coalitions are coordinated (unverified).
