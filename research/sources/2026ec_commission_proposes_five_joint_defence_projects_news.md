## Raw summary
EUR 325 m allocated from EDIP "as set out in the work programme published on 30 March 2026"; average 18 Member States per project, Ukraine in four of five; "The Commission is expected to take part in the EDPCIs, provide financial support and continue supporting Member States coordinate their implementation. It will also monitor progress against agreed milestones." Next steps: after Council adoption the EDPCIs become eligible "in accordance with a dedicated procedure"; funding enables initial deployment and lays foundation for possible funding via the future European Competitiveness Fund. Kubilius: combined funding ambition around EUR 190 bn by 2036.

## Key conclusions
- ✅ Confirms (again) the state-level, "dedicated procedure" funding path and that **the Commission itself coordinates/monitors alongside Member States**.
- ✅ **No industry call, supplier channel or industry dialogue is mentioned** for the EDPCIs.
