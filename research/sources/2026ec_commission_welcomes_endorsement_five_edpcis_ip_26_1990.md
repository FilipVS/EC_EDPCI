## Raw summary
"Following today's decision, the defence projects can now move towards funding." EUR 325 m allocated from EDIP for establishment and deployment. "Next steps: The Commission will now work closely with participating Member States to support the implementation of the projects and monitor progress towards their agreed objectives and milestones." EDPCIs are long-term and "extend beyond the current EDIP funding period"; other EDPCIs may be identified; Union support may continue under the next MFF, including the proposed European Competitiveness Fund. On average 18 Member States per project; Ukraine in four of five (as stated in the release; the Council annex lists Ukraine in all five — ⚠️ discrepancy between sources). Background line dates the proposal "3 July 2025" (⚠️ apparent typo; all other documents say 3 July 2026).

## Key conclusions
- ✅ The Commission's stated next step is working **with the participating Member States**; it announces **no call dates, workshops, info days or industry events** for the EDPCIs.
