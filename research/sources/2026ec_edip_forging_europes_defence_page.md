## Raw summary
- EDIP = EUR 1.5 bn; Ukraine Support Instrument EUR 300 m.
- Components: IRA >EUR 700 m; Common Procurement EUR 240 m (grants up to EUR 20 m/project to consortia of national contracting authorities; calls close Oct 2026 and Feb 2027); **EDPCI "EUR 325 million"**; Defence Innovation Action EUR 35.3 m; Equity Facility 2.0 EUR 100 m; JAQ EUR 50 m; SEAPs, security of supply, military sales catalogue.
- IRA for MS + Norway industry: **lump-sum grants up to 35 % of eligible costs, up to 50 % if increased-funding conditions met**. 1st call (Energetic Components, EUR 166.4 m) closed. **2nd call, deadline 16 Feb 2027**: (a) **Key Electronic Components EUR 122.25 m — "guidance electronics, propulsion electronics, RF and laser modules, multispectral cameras, avionics, PCBs and IC substrates, and Lithium-ion polymer batteries, power electronics and critical semiconductor building blocks"**, max EUR 20 m/project; (b) Platforms & End-products EUR 152.75 m, max EUR 30 m. USI-IRA (Ukraine) calls: 13 Oct 2026 and 16 Feb 2027.
- EDPCI section: five EDPCIs "will soon be eligible to receive financing opportunities"; "financing opportunities may be continued under the future EU MFF". Links to Commission proposal and five factsheets.

## Key conclusions
- ✅ **EUR 325 m for EDPCIs is an official Commission figure** (confirms Heuking's number; see WP factsheet for the 300 + 25 split). It is a total across all five, not per project, and "soon eligible" means no EDPCI call yet.
- ✅ **A company-facing call whose topic text names RF modules and lithium-ion polymer batteries exists (IRA Key Electronic Components, deadline 16 Feb 2027).** This is the first confirmed concrete instrument matching the user's product lines — it is EDIP/IRA, **not** an EDPCI call (do not conflate).
- ✅ Heuking's "Oct 2026 / Feb 2027" dates refer to **Common Procurement (national authorities)**, not companies — a correction to how Heuking's dates could be read.
