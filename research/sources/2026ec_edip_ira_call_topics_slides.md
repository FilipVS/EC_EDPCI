## Raw summary
- IRA topics: EDF-EDIP-P-2026-LS-IRA-EC (Energetic Components; Apr–Jun 2026; EUR 166.4 m; max EUR 30 m/project); **EDF-EDIP-P-2027-LS-IRA-KEC (Key Electronic Components; submission June 2026 – Feb 2027; EUR 122.25 m; max EUR 20 m/project incl. EUR 500 k optional WP)**; EDF-EDIP-P-2027-LS-IRA-PE (Platforms & End-products; EUR 152.75 m; max EUR 30 m).
- Proposals must centre on ≥1 targeted product AND ≥1 targeted production capacity; must show criticality, risk management, KPIs; minimum action size and max consortium size defined per topic; **single beneficiaries are eligible**; ongoing projects eligible if not started before 5 Mar 2024.
- KEC targeted products include UxS/loitering munitions, (counter-)UxS, missile defence, mobile comms/C2, **C4ISR incl. EW, radars, sonar, wideband multifunction RF systems**. KEC targeted capacities include **RF and laser modules (RF front-end, T/R modules, datalink modules, timing/frequency references)**; **propulsion electronics incl. battery management system electronics, power distribution**; **lithium-ion polymer batteries, power electronics, GaN/GaAs/SiGe/RF-CMOS supply chain**; PCBs/high-frequency laminates.

## Key conclusions
- ✅ **This is the first source that names batteries and RF explicitly**: IRA KEC covers both — and a single company can apply (no consortium obligatory ✅ per slide "single beneficiaries are eligible"). Overturns the earlier "no source mentions battery/RF" and partially weakens the "must join a consortium" hypothesis **for this instrument** (the hypothesis may still hold for EDPCIs themselves).
- ✅ Deadline per Commission page: 16 Feb 2027 (see `2026ec_edip_forging_europes_defence_page.md`).
