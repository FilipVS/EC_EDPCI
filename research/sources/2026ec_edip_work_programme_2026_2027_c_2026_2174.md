## Raw summary (EDPCI parts only)
- **Call EDIP-P-2026-FNLC-EDPCI, topic EDIP-P-2026-FNLC-EDPCI-STA "Short term activities related to the deployment of European Defence Projects of Common Interest."**
  - *Applicants targeted:* "A consortium of Member States or associated countries, **including at least four Member States**, participating in a European Defence Project of Common Interest established by a Council implementing act, or a SEAP established by a Commission implementing act and consisting of those Member States and associated countries." → **no company is an applicant.**
  - *Fundable activities (exhaustive, "only"):* (a) common procurement of defence products; (b) accelerating adjustment of production capacity and related supporting activities; (c) industrial development of new/upgraded defence products; (d) development and procurement of necessary infrastructure (= Reg. Art. 35(8)).
  - *What each proposal must state per activity:* expected scope, outcome, timeline, milestones, estimated financial envelope, **the legal entity (e.g. procurement agent) managing the activity**, the participating countries involved and their roles, the cooperation framework (e.g. LoI, MoU, contracts), and links with SAFE plans where relevant. Must show no duplication with other EU programmes (EDF, ASAP, EDIRPA, other EDIP actions).
  - *Implementation:* directly by DG DEFIS. *Budget:* "The Commission is **considering** a total budget of **EUR 300 000 000** for the call." Appendix 1 books the EUR 300 m in **2027** appropriations (nothing in 2026).
- **Topic EDIP-P-2026-FNLC-CSA-EDPCI** "Coordination and support action for developing the long-term implementation roadmap of an EDPCI, including its governance": applicants again ≥4 Member States/associated countries participating in an EDPCI (or a SEAP). Prepares deployment under the **next MFF (2028–2034)**: first roadmap version **by end 2027** (deployment activities 2028–2030), full roadmap **by end 2029**. Each roadmap must list all activities with expected outcomes, **cost estimates incl. expected EU financial support**, state of play and links to existing EU-funded projects, **participating countries and their responsibilities, the legal entity (e.g. procurement agent) for each activity**, cooperation framework (LoI/MoU/contracts), and how components fit the whole. All participating countries should be involved; EDA may join unfunded. Results "will not commit the Commission to provide the expected EU contribution under the next MFF." Budget **EUR 25 m** (Appendix 1: EUR 4.5 m in 2026 + EUR 20.5 m in 2027).
- **Appendices 2c (funding methods/rates), 3c (milestones and amounts) and 4c (verification arrangements) for EDPCI deployment each say only: "Further details as required by Article 21(3) of Regulation (EU) 2025/2643 will be provided at a later stage."** The same applies to the EDPCI call's additional information.
- No opening date, deadline or call timetable for the EDPCI call appears in the text I searched (⚠️ searched by keyword, not every page read).

## Key conclusions
- ✅ **This is the "dedicated procedure" in outline:** a Commission grant call (run by DG DEFIS) whose applicants are **consortia of ≥4 participating states** (or a SEAP), not companies; the EUR 300 m is earmarked for **2027** appropriations.
- ✅ **The companies enter through the proposal's content**: each activity must name the legal entity (e.g. procurement agent) and the cooperation framework. That is where industrial roles get fixed → a supplier needs to be known to those states/agents **before** the consortium drafts the proposal.
- ✅ The **EUR 25 m "for future projects under the next MFF"** (earlier Commission wording) = the CSA-EDPCI roadmap action; the roadmaps (end 2027 / end 2029) will contain cost estimates and the entities per activity — a second, longer-term point where suppliers become visible.
- ✅ **Still not published**: call opening/deadline, funding rate/method, milestones and verification for EDPCI deployment (all "later stage"). The EUR 300 m is "considered", not final.
- ⚠️ Inference: the earliest realistic timing of EDPCI grant awards is 2027 (budget year) — not stated as a date.
