# EDIP Work Programme factsheet (March 2026)

- Source: European Commission DG DEFIS, "The European Defence Industry Programme — Work Programme, March 2026" (PDF, creation date 27 Mar 2026). Official ✅. Fetched 2026-10-01 from https://defence-industry-space.ec.europa.eu/document/download/3864237c-ff5b-4f50-80e8-dd41a5b19426_en?filename=EDIP%20WP%20factsheet%20v4.pdf
- Attachment: `attachments/2026ec_edip_work_programme_factsheet.pdf` — ✅ read in full (2 pages, summary-level, not the legal WP).

## Raw summary
- WP allocates EUR 1.5 bn over 2026–2027 incl. EUR 300 m Ukraine.
- IRA EUR 700 m: EUR 440 m for EU/Norwegian industry (energetic components, **key electronic components**, platforms/end-products); EUR 260 m for Ukrainian + EU industry (unmanned/counter-unmanned; missiles/ammo/bombs).
- Common Procurement EUR 240 m, 5 topics incl. counter-drone, AMD, C5ISR/space products.
- **EDPCIs: "EUR 300 million to initiate the implementation of ambitious European defence projects. Additional EUR 25 million to support the development of future projects under the upcoming MFF"**; target: Member States and Norway.
- Other: Equity Facility EUR 100 m, JAQ EUR 50 m, SEAP support EUR 10 m, etc.

## Key conclusions
- ✅ EDPCI money = EUR 300 m + EUR 25 m = EUR 325 m, **aimed at Member States and Norway**, i.e. state-level — not directly company-addressable.
- ✅ Company-addressable money is in **IRA** (EUR 440 m for EU/Norway industry).
