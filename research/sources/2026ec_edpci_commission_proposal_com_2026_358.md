# Commission proposal COM(2026) 358 final — Council Implementing Decision identifying EDPCIs (3 Jul 2026)

- Source: European Commission, Brussels 3.7.2026, 2026/0192 (NLE). Official ✅. Fetched 2026-10-01 from https://defence-industry-space.ec.europa.eu/system/files/2026-07/Proposal-for-a-Council-Implementing-Decision-on-the-establishment-of-european-Projects-of-Common-Interest.pdf

## Raw summary
- Legal basis Art. 35(3) Reg. (EU) 2025/2643. Nine MS proposals (deadline 29 May 2026) → five proposed; four others (ERCUL, ARTISYS, ARCADIA, EDDS) found not yet mature/clear enough, may get EDIP funding to develop and be reconsidered after a later call for expression of interest.
- Budget section: EDPCI funding "may be provided by the budgetary envelope identified for that purpose in the Work Programme adopted pursuant to Article 21"; decision "without prejudice to any subsequent decision to award Union funding". After establishment, the participating MS group (and possible future SEAPs) "would be eligible and granted funding under Article 35 … in accordance with a dedicated procedure".
- Annex per project: country lists, estimated sizes (same Czechia pattern as Council draft).

## Key conclusions
- ✅ Czechia listed in DECODER, SPACE, EU-FIAMD, EFW; not IMSD — **identical to Council draft**, so the CZ conclusion is stable across both stages.
- ✅ **Country lists grew between Commission proposal and Council draft**: SPACE 16→20, EU-FIAMD 16→19, EFW 15→17 (DECODER 28 and IMSD 17 listed in both). Czechia stable. (Heuking's remark that lists changed is thereby confirmed ✅.)
- ✅ **EFW size differs**: proposal says EUR 60–100 bn by 2036; Council draft/factsheet say EUR 8.5–10 bn by 2032 and up to EUR 100 bn by 2036. Use the later Council/Commission-factsheet wording; flag the difference.
- ✅ Funding for EDPCIs goes to the **participating Member States group / SEAP via a "dedicated procedure"** — not a company call. Companies would come in downstream (procurement/ramp-up).
- ✅ Scope text of EFW includes "SMEs and mid-caps integration via supply chain diversification" — the only explicit SME hook in the five projects ⚠️ (rationale text, not an entry mechanism).
