## Raw summary
Summarises the Roadmap. Reports that before the Dec 2025 European Council, **Germany, France and Italy opposed endorsing the flagships at EU level**, so leaders only "took stock of ongoing work on Member State-led capability coalitions"; many governments favour ad hoc coalitions. Notes the Commission's Tech Alliances launch (28 Nov 2025) and the planned Defence Industrial Summit by mid-2026; ASD supports the roadmap, trade unions critical.

## Key conclusions
- ⚠️ Context: political uncertainty about the flagships in Dec 2025, since overtaken by the Council decision of 28 Sep 2026 identifying five EDPCIs.
- Confirms the industry-dialogue milestones are in the Roadmap; says nothing on whether they happened.
