## Summary (claims by the author)
- Combined funding ambition "around EUR 190 billion by 2036"; "Commission has allocated EUR 325 million from the EUR 1.5 billion EDIP budget to support their establishment and deployment"; Ukraine Support Instrument EUR 260 m for collaborative projects.
- Eligibility: recipients established in EU/associated country with executive management there; non-associated-country-controlled firms only with guarantees.
- Supply chain: components from outside EU/associated countries generally **≤35 %** of component cost of end product; "design authority" rule (ITAR/EAR relevance).
- Procurement: common procurement by participating countries via a procurement agent appointed **by unanimity**; contracting authorities may be MS, associated countries, international orgs, SEAPs or EDA.
- Mentions BraveTechEU (EDIP SME/start-up initiative); IP/classified-information guarantees; state-aid, competition, export-control cautions; permitting "overriding public interest" for production facilities.
- Says "first call closing October 2026, second call closing February 2027" for upcoming EDIP funding procedures.
- Advice list: map capabilities, identify primes/MS sponsors/procurement agents, audit 35% threshold, ITAR/EAR, consortium docs.
