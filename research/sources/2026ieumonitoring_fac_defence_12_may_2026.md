## Raw summary
Defence ministers discussed implementation of the defence readiness agenda; the discussion was **preceded by a presentation by the president of ASD (Aerospace, Security and Defence Industries Association of Europe), named in the text as Micael Johansson**; Kallas: "We also had the defence industry representatives present … the industry representatives were then explaining what is hindering them from ramping up." She also said production is "still not ramped up".
