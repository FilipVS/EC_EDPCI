## Raw summary
- Defence ministers "agreed to fund the Eastern Flank Watch initiative". Minister Robertas Kaunas: "The EU will provide **over EUR 62 million in funding in the short-term**…"; Lithuania will seek sufficient and sustainable funding in the next MFF (2028–2034).
- "Lithuania is one of the member states that initiated the Eastern Flank Watch project. **Alongside Finland, Estonia, Latvia, Poland and Sweden, Lithuania presented a joint project proposal to the European Commission in May this year.** The number of participating member states later grew to 18."

## Key conclusions
- ✅ **First named initiators of any EDPCI**: Eastern Flank Watch — Lithuania, Finland, Estonia, Latvia, Poland, Sweden (joint proposal, May 2026, in answer to the Commission's call for expressions of interest). Czechia is not among the initiators named; it is a later participant (Council annex lists it).
- ⚠️ **"over EUR 62 million short-term" is unverified**: no Commission or Council document I read contains it (the Work Programme considers EUR 300 m for all five projects, booked in 2027 appropriations, with funding methods "at a later stage"). Do not use the figure.
