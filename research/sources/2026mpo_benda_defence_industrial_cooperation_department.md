# MPO press release: Matěj Benda appointed head of Defence-Industrial Cooperation department (14 Sep 2026)

## Summary (translated)
- As of 14 Sep 2026 Matěj Benda is director of the new **Odbor obranně-průmyslové spolupráce** (Defence-Industrial Cooperation Dept) of MPO.
- Defence-industry agenda is new at MPO, **transferred with effect from 1 Jul 2026**. The department sits in the **Sekce Evropské unie a zahraničního obchodu** (EU and Foreign Trade Section); keeps ties with Ministry of Defence and the Czech Army.
- Support "on several pillars": export and foreign activities, support of industrial cooperation, **support in the area of European funds and innovation**; MPO will offer existing infrastructure to Czech defence industry. Key: cooperation with MoD.
- Benda: at MPO 10+ years (6 in managerial roles); PhD international law, focus EU law; recently deputy director of the Licensing Administration (control of foreign trade in sensitive items and sanctions); involved in EU/national dual-use and military-material norms and EU outreach projects (ATT, dual-use).

## Key conclusions
- ✅ The department exists and covers "European funds" support for Czech defence industry; Benda is its director.
- ⚠️ **The release does not mention EDPCI, EDIP, or any national contact-point role.** "MPO dept = Czech EDPCI contact point" is the user's hypothesis, plausible but **not confirmed** by this source.
