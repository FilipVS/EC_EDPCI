## Raw summary
- **Two calls** (one per region category): less-developed regions CZK 863,869,998; transition regions CZK 986,130,002; receipt to 15 Oct 2026 15:00; MPO may end receipt per region category at 300 % of allocation, earliest 31 Jul 2026. Individual projects; regional investment aid under **GBER Art. 14**.
- Supported: productive investment into development and **production** of critical technologies: production lines, first-of-a-kind equipment, expansion/change of use of existing facilities, scaling processes, quality control; value chain incl. final products, specific components and specific machines. Example given for STEP: equipment for **battery** manufacturing. Sectors: digital/deep tech (advanced semiconductors, AI, quantum, **advanced connectivity/navigation, advanced sensing**, robotics), clean tech (**battery technologies and energy storage**), biotech, **defence tech** (items on the EU Common Military List; Council capability areas incl. air & missile defence, drones/counter-drone, **cyber/AI/electronic warfare**, military mobility). RF is not named explicitly.
- Applicant: Czech legal/natural person with IČ, two tax periods, ownership-transparency declarations, no sanctions link, not in difficulty. **Territory: Czechia excluding Prague**; place of implementation decides. Grant min CZK 5 m, max CZK 250 m. **Recipient must contribute ≥25 % of eligible costs** from own or external non-state-aid sources; payment ex post; VAT pre-financed. Physical completion by **30 Jun 2029**; project must not start before application registration; asset kept 5 years (3 for SMEs); TRL 4+; one project per IČ.
- **Large (non-SME) enterprises in Střední Čechy, Jihozápad, Jihovýchod**: only initial investment for a **new economic activity** (different 4-digit NACE class).
- Support intensity table (EU share; rest from recipient): e.g. Severozápad 75/70/60 % (small/medium/large); Severovýchod 60/50/40; Střední Morava 60/50/40; Moravskoslezsko 70/60/50; transition-region districts mostly 40/30/20, with 50/40/30 for Kladno, Rakovník, Mělník, Plzeň-sever, Tachov.
- Less-developed regions = NUTS II Severozápad, Severovýchod, Střední Morava, Moravskoslezsko; transition regions = Střední Čechy, Jihozápad, Jihovýchod (stated in the text). Mapping to kraje from API's regional map (user-independent): transition = Středočeský, Plzeňský, Jihočeský, Vysočina, Jihomoravský; Prague excluded.

## Key conclusions
- ✅ **This is the only Czech instrument found that funds production lines (incl. battery manufacturing equipment) directly**; it fits a battery maker, and defence electronics/sensing/EW partially (RF not explicit).
- ✅ **Only transition-region locations remain** (MRR half closed 1 Aug 2026); location of the physical investment — not the registered seat — decides eligibility.
- ✅ Heavy conditions: 25 % own contribution, ex-post payment, ≥CZK 5 m grant, large firms need a new NACE activity in these regions, five-year retention.
- ⚠️ Open to 15 Oct 2026 on paper; practical availability depends on whether the 300 % cap was hit.
