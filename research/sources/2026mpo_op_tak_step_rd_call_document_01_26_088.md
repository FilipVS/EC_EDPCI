## Raw summary
- Receipt 17 Jul 2026 – 30 Sep 2026 09:59:59; allocation CZK 1.850 bn; MPO may close receipt once submitted applications reach **300 % of allocation, not before 10 Aug 2026** (→ happened 11 Aug, per API). State aid: GBER Art. 25; Czech IČ required; **supported: industrial research and experimental development only; "výrobní aktivity" (production) are listed as NOT supported (4.2c).** Call is meant for "non-production activities of the value chain".
- Scope = STEP sectors. Clean tech list includes **"Bateriové technologie a technologie skladování energie"**; footnote cites next-generation batteries/supercapacitors. Defence technologies = technologies built into products on the EU Common Military List (Dir. 2009/43/EC) and tied to Council priority capability areas: air and missile defence, artillery/precision strike, missiles and munitions, **drones and counter-drone systems, cyber security/AI/electronic warfare**, military mobility, ground/naval/air combat, health. **Advanced sensing** is listed under digital/deep tech.
- Two STEP conditions (innovative/emerging/cutting-edge: ≥2 elements; or strategic-dependency reduction with documented EU strategy basis). STEP Seal projects get fast-track (automatic 93 points; only budget criterion scored).
- **International projects not supported**; consortia of Czech applicants/partners (Czech IČ) accepted; max 5 members; research organisation only as partner with financial contribution; one application per applicant; deliverable ≥ one of: pilot plant, verified technology, prototype, functional sample, digital solution; TRL 4+ (per API page).
- Applicant: Czech legal entity with business licence, tax resident ≥ two tax periods, no Russia-sanctions link, not in difficulty, etc.; **target territory: Czechia excluding NUTS 2 Prague**.
- Rates: industrial research 70/60/50 % (small/medium/large), experimental development 45/35/25 %; with effective collaboration up to 80/75/65 % and 60/50/40 %; 85 % for research organisations; grant min CZK 10 m, max CZK 150 m. Eligible costs: external services ≤30 %, personnel, materials, 15 % overhead, depreciation ≤20 %.

## Key conclusions
- ✅ This call was **R&D, not production**; therefore it is **not** the instrument for scaling battery/RF manufacturing. That role belongs to **STEP – Investice** (separate call; not supplied).
- ✅ Batteries and drone/counter-drone/EW technologies were explicitly in scope; RF is covered only indirectly (advanced sensing, EW, Common Military List technologies).
- ✅ Cannot be used for cross-border consortia (Czech-only), unlike EDIP.
- ✅ The call closed 11 Aug 2026 after hitting the 300 % cap (per API).
