## Raw summary
- Call **STEP – Investice** (OP TAK, EU cohesion funds), development **and production** of critical technologies in four STEP sectors: digital/deep tech, clean tech, biotech, **defence technologies**. Two technical calls by region type: less-developed regions CZK 863.87 m, transition regions CZK 986.13 m (total ≈ CZK 1.85 bn).
- **Applications 17 Jul 2026 – 15 Oct 2026** via ISKP2021+. Intermediary: Agentura pro podnikání a inovace (hotline 800 800 777).
- Critical technology must be consistent with STEP and either bring an innovative, emerging, cutting-edge element with significant single-market potential, or reduce/prevent EU strategic dependency.
