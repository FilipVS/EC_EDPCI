## Raw summary
- Industrial research and experimental development of critical technologies (same four STEP sectors incl. defence); all company sizes, alone or in consortia; large enterprises may now apply alone. STEP Seal holders get fast-track assessment.
- Headline allocation CZK 1.850 bn. **Applications 17 Jul 2026 – 30 Sep 2026**; may close earlier once applications reach 300 % of allocation, earliest 10 Aug 2026.
