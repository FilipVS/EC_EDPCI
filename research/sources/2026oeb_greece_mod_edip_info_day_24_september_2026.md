## Raw summary
The Hellenic Ministry of National Defence "is planning to organize" an **Information Day on EDIP** on **24 Sep 2026** at the Armed Forces Officers' Club (LAED), Athens, combined with a Greek-Cypriot Defence Industry Day, focusing on "the latest developments regarding the EDIP programme and the **European Defence Projects of Common Interest (EDPCI)**" and on Greek–Cypriot industry cooperation. Cypriot defence companies were asked to register their representatives by 31 Aug 2026 with the Cyprus MoD's defence-industry office (defence.industry@mod.gov.cy). No agenda or speakers listed. The page uses future tense, so whether the event took place as planned is unconfirmed.

## Key conclusions
- ✅ **The only EDPCI-related industry event found anywhere** — and it is a **national ministry initiative** (Greece), not a Commission one. It predates the Council decision (28 Sep 2026) by four days.
- ⚠️ Useful as a model: a Member State MoD briefing its own industry on EDPCIs and organising industry-to-industry contacts. No equivalent Czech event found.
