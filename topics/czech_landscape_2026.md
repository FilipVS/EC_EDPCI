# What exists in Czechia today — instruments, institutions, deadlines (as of 1 Oct 2026)

Evidence: ✅ official/Czech-government or EU source; ⚠️ association, consultancy, news or search snippet. Programme families kept separate (EDIP ≠ EDF ≠ national).

## Institutions
- ✅ **MPO** — Odbor obranně-průmyslové spolupráce, director **Matěj Benda** (since 14 Sep 2026); MPO took over the defence-industry agenda from MoD (✅ MPO: 1 Jul 2026; ⚠️ a news site says 1 Jun). Per AOBP (⚠️ relaying official procedure), MPO now issues **EDF Letters of Intent and security guarantees** (contact matej.benda@mpo.gov.cz, data box bxtaaw4) — *EDF only; no source applies this to EDIP/EDPCI*.
- ✅ **MoD** keeps military requirements and acquisitions; the General Staff judges military usability (⚠️ per AOBP/news).
- ✅ **CzechInvest Defence Hub** — contact point for DIANA/NIF/EDF (startup-oriented; no EDIP mention).
- ⚠️ **AOBP** — industry association and EDF National Focal Point (edf@aobp.cz). No EDIP focal point found.
- ⚠️ **Not found anywhere:** a Czech-designated EDIP/EDPCI contact point, Czech lead entities in any EDPCI, Czech co-funding for EDPCIs, or a Czech national position paper on EDPCI. A Czech commentator (29 Sep 2026) explicitly asks for such information → likely not yet published (inference ⚠️).

## Key conclusions
- ✅ Czechia is a listed participant in 4 of 5 EDPCIs, but **no Czech industry-facing EDPCI mechanism is public yet**.
- ✅ The only open instruments that a battery/RF company can use *now or soon*: **IRA-KEC (16 Feb 2027)** and **OP TAK STEP – Investice (15 Oct 2026)**. Everything else found is closed or not yet published.
- ✅ The sister **R&D** call closed 11 Aug (~3× oversubscribed) and excluded production. **STEP – Investice** is the production route: its less-developed-regions half closed 1 Aug 2026; the **transition-regions half** (5 kraje listed above; location of the physical investment decides) is listed open to 15 Oct 2026 but may close earlier if it reaches 300 % (⚠️ unverified). Large (non-SME) firms there must invest in a *new* economic activity (new 4-digit NACE class).
- **Not required: EU-level consortium for IRA-KEC** ✅, but Czech/other national support top-ups for IRA co-financing: not found.
