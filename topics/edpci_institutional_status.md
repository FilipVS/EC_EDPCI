# What EDPCI actually is (institutional status)

Evidence: ✅ = official; ⚠️ = secondary/inferred.

## Confirmed
- ✅ EDPCI = "European Defence Project of Common Interest", a status created by **Art. 35 of Regulation (EU) 2025/2643 (EDIP Regulation, 16 Dec 2025)**. [Council draft ST 12742/26]
- ✅ Process: Commission call for expressions of interest to **Member States** (16 Feb 2026) → 9 MS proposals (by 29 May 2026) → HR/EDA input → Commission proposal of 5 (3 Jul 2026) → **Council implementing decision, approved 28 Sep 2026**. [Council PR; ST 12742/26]
- ✅ Entry into force of the decision: day after OJ publication (draft text). Final OJ text not yet seen.
- ✅ Effect: the 5 are "now **eligible** to receive EU funding through the EDIP", seeding "possible further funding via the upcoming European Competitiveness Fund". [Council PR]
- ✅ Commission may itself participate; participating MS may make HR and EDA observers. [ST 12742/26 recital 7]
- ✅ Second call for expressions of interest (new EDPCIs) intended **before end April 2027**. [ST 12742/26 recital 6]

## Not confirmed / not yet published
- Per-EDPCI EU money, EDPCI funding call, deadlines → **still not published** (Commission page: EDPCIs "will soon be eligible to receive financing opportunities").
- ~~Heuking's EUR 325 m unverified~~ **Corrected 2026-10-01**: ✅ official — EDIP WP factsheet: EUR 300 m + EUR 25 m for future projects under next MFF; Commission page: EUR 325 m. Total for all EDPCIs, aimed at Member States/Norway. Heuking's "Oct 2026 / Feb 2027" dates are the **Common Procurement** calls (national authorities), per the Commission page — not EDPCI calls.
- Competitiveness Fund: "upcoming", not yet existing → not yet published.
- Whether companies can "apply to join" an EDPCI: **no source says so.** The structure documented is state-level: participating *countries* and (per Heuking ⚠️) common procurement through a procurement agent appointed by participating states.

## Added 2026-10-01 from the Regulation text ✅ (`research/sources/2025eu_edip_regulation_2025_2643.md`)
- Art. 35(13): **only Member States, associated countries and SEAPs are eligible for EDPCI funding.** Companies are not funding recipients; they contract with the states/procurement agent.
- Art. 20(1): EDPCI grants may cover up to 100 % of eligible costs. Art. 3(4): up to 25 % of the EUR 1.2 bn envelope (= EUR 300 m) may go to EDPCIs.
- Art. 35(19): later accession of any MS/associated country/Ukraine needs approval of all participating MS. Art. 35(15): national support schemes allowed.

## Assessment
EDPCI is an **EU Council-identified, member-state-driven framework under EDIP** — real legal status, but the identification itself carries **no binding funding commitment and no company-facing call**. The headline "EUR 3.5–100 bn" figures are *estimated overall sizes of the projects* (largely national spending), not EU funding. Do not present EDPCI as a funding pot.
