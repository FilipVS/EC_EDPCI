- ✅ **No EDPCI grant call is open yet** and none has a date (Work Programme C(2026) 2174: call defined, EUR 300 m "considered", booked in 2027 appropriations; rates/milestones/verification "at a later stage"). ## Assessment
EDPCI is at the **state-coordination stage**: participating states are organising themselves (initiators, procurement agents, cooperation frameworks) before any call opens. There is no public workshop or supplier process yet; any supplier access will have to come through those states or their agents.
