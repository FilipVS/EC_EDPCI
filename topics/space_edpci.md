# SPACE EDPCI — what the sources say (status 8 Oct 2026)

Tiers: ✅ official; ⚠️ analyst/inference. **Czechia is a listed participant** (Commission proposal and Council draft) ✅.

## Official scope and framing
- **Council annex** (`research/sources/2026consilium_edpci_implementing_decision_draft_st12742.md` ✅ pre-adoption draft; same text in the proposal): "bolster European space capabilities" in space-based early warning (SBEW), satellite communications (SATCOM), ISR including signals intelligence (SIGINT), space domain awareness (SDA), positioning, navigation and timing (PNT) and navigation warfare (NAVWAR), responsive space systems (RSS), and "a multi-mission approach"; "proposes **investigating** the development of a multi-mission constellation"; focus on **common procurement of defence products incl. necessary infrastructure** in support of common military planning tools for SATCOM sharing, PNT services, ISR capabilities, NAVWAR detection and denial; further development of space capabilities for independent decision-making.
- **Commission rationale** (`research/sources/2026ec_edpci_commission_proposal_com_2026_358.md` ✅): supports "the transformation of mature R&D into sovereign operational capabilities that no single Member State can develop alone", broadens cross-border partnerships, creates new market opportunities, reduces structural dependence in space; "a direct and structural contribution to the **EU Space Shield**"; takes into account relevant PESCO projects; benefits extend Union-wide.
- **Size:** "up to EUR 24 billion by 2034" — identical in proposal, Council draft and the Commission factsheet (`research/sources/2026ec_edpci_factsheet_space.md`); the only project with an "up to" figure (no lower bound).
- **Press wording** (Council, 28 Sep 2026 — `research/sources/2026consilium_edpci_first_five_projects.md` ✅): "early-warning satellites to detect missile launches, satellite communications, intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance, … precise positioning and timing systems … navigation warfare and the rapid launch of replacement satellites".

## Participants
- Commission proposal (3 Jul): 16 countries — BE, CZ, DK, DE, IE, EL, ES, FR, IT, LU, NL, PL, RO, FI, SE + NO.
- Council draft (14 Sep): 20 — adds BG, CY, HU, AT and Ukraine (18 MS + NO + UA).
- ⚠️ Discrepancy: the Commission's 28 Sep press release says Ukraine takes part in **four of five** projects (consistent with the proposal, where SPACE had no Ukraine), while the Council draft lists Ukraine in SPACE. Check the Official Journal text.
- **No initiators, lead nation, constellation design, procurement agent or timeline found.**

## Policy origin: the Roadmap's "European Space Shield" ✅
(`research/sources/2025ec_defence_readiness_roadmap_2030_joint_communication.md`) — to "set up and protect a comprehensive European system of space capabilities that serve defence purposes", complementing EU dual-use space capabilities (**Galileo Public Regulated Service**, Earth-Observation Governmental Service, **IRIS²** secure communications); "focusing notably on **Galileo-enabled equipment, Space Domain Awareness, countering jamming and spoofing, and in-space operations and services**, where Europe has clear shortfalls and dependencies". Milestones: EUCO endorsement end 2025; coordinate Space/IRIS²/EDF/EDIP work programmes and Member States' procurement plans (SAFE) from 2026; launch Q2 2026. The EDPCI SPACE scope is broader (SBEW, SATCOM, ISR/SIGINT) than this list.
- Related but separate: on 28 Sep 2026 the Council also strengthened the EU space threat response architecture and set up a space security toolbox (`research/sources/2026consilium_timeline_european_defence_readiness.md` ✅).

## Analyst context (⚠️ pre-adoption EP study, `research/sources/2026blockmans_edpci_from_concept_to_practice.md`)
Space is an enabler without which the drone, air-defence and eastern-flank projects "will be impossible"; challenges: crowded orbit and counter-space threats, national fragmentation, sovereign launch choices; names European space primes (Airbus Defence and Space, OHB, Indra, Thales Alenia Space, ArianeGroup) and existing PESCO/EDF space projects — different programme families, not EDPCI facts.

No source mentions "RF" or "battery". RF-heavy items implied by the text: **SIGINT, NAVWAR detection/denial, jamming/spoofing countermeasures, Galileo PRS-enabled equipment, SATCOM** (terminals, payloads, ground segment). Batteries only for space-grade/satellite power — niche.
